On the ground

School, IEC and partner visits are planned, logged and mapped city by city, with the counselor's own contact details captured at the table.

02

Never lost

Calls, meetings and follow-ups between visits are recorded against the same counselor, so the relationship survives a change of staff on either side.

03

Travel, stay and event spend is requested, approved and reconciled against uploaded bills. Campus sees exactly what the desk cost and what it produced.
